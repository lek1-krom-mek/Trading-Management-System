§ 03

Why Model B

Three structural advantages
i.

Lower psychological weight

One stopped trade in Model A ends the day emotionally. In Model B, a single loss is one-third of allocated risk — the next confirmed setup remains executable without revenge pressure.

ii.

Native SOP alignment

The Spirit of the Unit protocol allows up to three trades daily. Model B uses that allocation properly — every trade still requires all 8 SOP rules satisfied, no forcing.

iii.

Adaptive opportunity capture

Low-confluence days take 1 trade. High-conviction NY killzone sessions take 2–3. Model A misses the second and third clean LOBs that appear naturally in active sessions.

⊹ Final assessment ⊹

Distribution outperforms
concentration at every account size.

The mathematical edge per dollar of risk is identical between models — what differs is execution quality, emotional sustainability, and protocol fit. Model B preserves daily loss limits while spreading capital across confirmed signals. For a discretionary trader operating under the Spirit of the Unit protocol, Model B is the structurally correct choice.
